A little about me

Hello, I'm Elissia Palmer. I'm an artist who loves bringing ideas, characters, and stories to life through illustration. My work is inspired by my imagination, the world around me, stories that have influenced me throughout my life, and most importantly my faith. Outside of my artistic work, I enjoy hiking, listening to music, playing video games, watching movies, and reading. These interests often influence my imagination and serve as inspiration for my artwork.
